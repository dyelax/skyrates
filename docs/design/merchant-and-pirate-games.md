# What keeps a captain sailing?

Design research for Skyrates, 24 November 2026. The first voyage has three decisions worth protecting. What can I carry that somebody needs? What am I willing to risk to get it there? Who will remember the delivery? Seven games illuminate different parts of those decisions.

The milestones establish scale or reception. They span different dates, business models and measures and cannot produce a defensible all-time sales ranking. The following named player accounts were read individually. They illustrate experiences players can have. Estimating how often those experiences occur would require a larger, systematically drawn sample.

## Seven designs at the helm

### Tradewinds gives a cargo destination a face

Classic’s distributor promises a fortune from a short circuit of ports, with pirate bounties as another livelihood. Sandlot’s own *Legends* description already includes skyships. Its reviewer found that a merchant, a fighter and a quest follower could fund themselves differently. Their praise turned to frustration on the iPhone when adjacent buildings and purchase fields repeatedly stole taps. In *Caravans*, Twinkletoes singles out differences among eastern, central and western markets and wanting to find out what happened to the characters. An airship alone is familiar territory. A gardener who knows the scions, pays for them, and later works in their shade gives this circuit an identity. [Sandlot pitch](https://www.interactive.org/games/video_game_details.asp?idGame=178) · [Gamezebo on Legends](https://www.gamezebo.com/reviews/tradewinds-legends-review/) · [Pocket Gamer on Tradewinds 2](https://www.pocketgamer.com/tradewinds-2/review/) · [Twinkletoes, 13 October 2021](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198282969976/recommended/36130/)

### Sid Meier’s Pirates! lets skill rearrange a career

Firaxis designer Jeff Briggs describes deliberately dropping chores that interrupted the pirate fantasy. The published PC manual connects crew complement, sail damage, grappling, and a captain’s duel. GameSpot describes players changing a captured town’s allegiance. The Steel Chicken remembers a small starting ship taking on a much bigger opponent and the freedom that followed. GolovA reports the opposite tactical experience, where boarding every ship worked and each town offered the same handful of goods. Let a clever, undergunned captain win. Then make the next ship’s crew, cargo and politics change which victory is worthwhile. [Firaxis designer diary](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/sid-meiers-pirates-designer-diary-2/1100-6102516/) · [PC manual](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/3920/manuals/manual.pdf?t=1568748025) · [GameSpot PC review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/sid-meiers-pirates-review/1900-6114066/) · [The Steel Chicken, 21 March 2015](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198083928547/recommended/3920/) · [GolovA, 19 September 2016](https://www.metacritic.com/game/sid-meiers-pirates/user-reviews/)

### Black Flag makes the next upgrade worth fighting for

Ubisoft’s pre-release pitch gives Edward immediate obligations to his purse and crew. In Tom Senior’s review, a broadside disables a ship, the grapples draw its deck alongside, recovered materials improve the Jackdaw, and the next opponent presents a harder boarding problem. Slowing to turn changes what the next shot can do. The same reviewer found the separate fleet-management battle game weak. A player who completed the game loved legendary ships and arsenal upgrades, then found an improved ship could erase ordinary threats almost instantly. Preserve the continuous approach, fire, contact and boarding sequence. The second cannon should change the angle a captain chooses. A larger damage number alone offers a much shorter learning curve. [Ubisoft’s May 2013 preview](https://blog.playstation.com/2013/05/15/new-assassins-creed-iv-black-flag-gameplay-trailer-takes-to-the-high-seas/) · [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/assassins-creed-4-black-flag-review/) · [Steam completion review, 18 October 2017](https://steamcommunity.com/id/skagner/recommended/242050/)

### Sea of Thieves puts another person’s job in sight

Rare’s early pitch was a shared-world adventure. In the prototype, the crew’s teamwork was visible even with stick figures. GameSpot’s launch review describes the strongest learning under fire, when somebody must hold course while another reloads or patches holes. Repetitive contracts and the loss of a whole haul could drain that enthusiasm. Shpydar describes a family crew with one player at the helm and another on the guns, willing to drop their planned voyage to help a stranger. Skyrates can give a solo captain that division of labor. Show the veteran moving to a grappling post and the gunner calling a reload, then let each report tell the player something they can act on. Preserve a captain’s earned relationships after a loss. [Rare’s reveal retrospective](https://www.seaofthieves.com/news/archive/looking-back-at-e3) · [Joe Neate on the prototype](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/podcast/733-fps-boost-apex-and-sea-of-thieves-celebrate-anniversaries/) · [GameSpot launch review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/sea-of-thieves-review-set-sail-with-trepidation/1900-6416887/) · [Shpydar, 22 June 2021](https://steamcommunity.com/id/shpydar/recommended/1172620/)

### Patrician III connects the manifest to the council

Its publisher offers election to office through commerce and investment in public works. Thorn Jackson found it rewarding to watch towns and populations grow, then let established routes run on their own. GameSpot describes specifying convoy price limits after the player learns the ports, but flags an important omission. Those rules cannot check a changing purchase cost, so an apparently solid route still needs supervision. Give captains a receipt with the actual cost of the units delivered. Let the consequence be something the port does on their next visit. Doing the same mathematics for every crate becomes less interesting once the route is understood. [Publisher description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/33570/Patrician_III/) · [GameSpot review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/patrician-iii-review/1900-6086007/) · [Thorn Jackson, 22 April 2015](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198070542305/recommended/33570/)

### Anno 1800 makes a missing input visible

The developers’ announcement asks what kind of industrial society the player will build. GameSpot describes the pleasure of following demand from a household to a farm or workshop. Multiple interconnected requirements eventually become hard to diagnose. General Public recounts running ships back and forth after their original need disappeared, then discovering a shipyard power requirement only after building costly infrastructure. A Skyrates producer should name the missing input and the reason at the place where a captain discovers the problem. The chart can then preserve the lead and its observation date. Show the nursery and its empty rack alongside the figures so a walk through port can explain a price. [Studio announcement](https://www.anno-union.com/anno1800-announcement/) · [GameSpot review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/anno-1800-review-the-prettiest-spreadsheet/1900-6417131/) · [General Public, 27 February 2023](https://steamcommunity.com/profiles/76561198929611996/recommended/916440/)

### Sunless Sea makes a story valuable enough to cross the map

Failbetter pitches officers with histories the captain can draw out while isolation and dwindling stores threaten the voyage. GameSpot found a personal search for a father’s bones more effective at guiding exploration than a wealth target. Repeating safe trips to rebuild the means to explore could choke off the new content. Two PC players, notwist and Meeleepit, describe how the drain of operating expenses or the confusing opening delayed the story they wanted. Give a Skyrates character a reason to trust the captain with the next question. The freeform conversation can deepen the answer. A missed conversational cue should leave the essential contract terms, destination and reward discoverable in the world. [Failbetter’s pitch](https://www.failbettergames.com/games/sunless-sea/) · [GameSpot review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/sunless-sea-review/1900-6416086/) · [Player accounts, June 2021 and September 2020](https://www.metacritic.com/game/sunless-sea/user-reviews/)

## A playtest on the Wayfarer

Run the first-session tasks with eight people new to Skyrates. Split mouse and touch evenly. Record actions and ask for reasons after the decision so prompting cannot teach the answer. These are proposed thresholds for a formative test. Small samples expose failures that a later, larger test can quantify.

| Moment | What to test | What would change the design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before leaving Sunstep | Give the player Vale’s offer. Ask them to choose a course. At least 6 of 8 can explain the 240-crown advance, identify who pays the 300-crown settlement, and depart knowing the galley is stocked. | If a player buys provisions out of fear of starvation, put the galley status on the gangway. If they call the invoice a donation, move the signed settlement terms into Vale’s offer. |
| Choosing a market | Ask where two pear grafts could help, then let the player look around. At least 6 of 8 choose Copperfall and name the winter garden without consulting a wiki. | If everyone follows a bare price number, change what the orchard porter says and what the gardener visibly does before adding more goods. |
| Finding a person | Ask each player to approach two named people or institutions. At least 14 of 16 first selections reach the intended target on mouse and touch combined. A mistaken selection must be recoverable in one action. | Separate overlapping hit areas, cue the intended speaker on approach, and put the person’s name on touch focus. |
| First combat | After instruction, stage a fresh approach. At least 6 of 8 evade an aimed round and score a shot with the single mounted gun. Ask each to point to the gun, its firing side, and the opponent before boarding. | If they can repeat keys but cannot find the firing lane, revise facing marks, projectile contrast and the crew’s call before changing difficulty. |
| A second encounter | Offer a lightly crewed courier and a well-crewed cutter in otherwise comparable encounters. Ask each captain to choose whether to board. At least 6 of 8 cite crew or hull as a reason to change tactics. | If ramming both always pays, alter crew signals, boarding risk or prize condition. Keep one prize worth taking intact. |
| The return passage | After a delivery and one change of season, show a dated quote for the same route. At least 6 of 8 distinguish an old observation from a current posting and name a local reason demand could change. | If they perceive arbitrary price noise, surface the current crop, frost or pressure condition on the quay and date the chart report beside the price. |
| The relationship | Offer a captain the same character before and after aiding the town. At least 6 of 8 identify one specific way the relationship changed. Essential directions and payment terms must remain obtainable after a digression or an offline session. | If the player just repeats a dialogue prompt to unlock a confidence, tie trust to a witnessed action and give the character a different public line on return. |

Track what happens after the reward. If a captain can name a person they are choosing to visit next, a trade route has become part of their own story.
